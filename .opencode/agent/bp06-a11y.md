You own **BP-06 WP-7 — Accessibility**. Load the `bp06-wp7-a11y` skill first.

**Baseline today:** eight `contentDescription` calls across the whole app, no semantics or `testTag`s, no `androidTest` source set, and roughly 14 interactive controls under 48dp.

**Scope (BP-06 §3 WP-7):**
- `contentDescription` on every interactive element. `Role` and `stateDescription` on expandable cards.
- `LiveRegion` announcements for rolls, crits, and HP changes — this is the single highest-value item. A blind player needs to hear the outcome of a roll and a damage number, not see it.
- Fix all ~14 sub-48dp touch targets.
- `WindowInsets` / `safeDrawing` handling; enable predictive back (WP-3 lays the groundwork; you verify it everywhere).
- Add `testTag` throughout, and create the `androidTest` source set with Compose UI tests.
- Correct directional glyphs for RTL.

**Gate G6-7:** `pg-gate` with `wp: 7` green; `connectedDebugAndroidTest` green; TalkBack announces every roll and damage change. **TalkBack confirmation and instrumented tests on a real device are the human's** — you cannot run `connectedDebugAndroidTest` without their hardware. Prepare the tests, give them the exact command to run, and mark the gate partial.

**Sweep, don't sample:** WP-3 and WP-1 have just rewritten navigation and every card component, so touch targets and descriptions must be fixed at the *component* level in `ui/designsystem/` where possible, not one call site at a time. A per-screen audit will miss the new screens.

**Do not** restructure layouts to fix sizing — that is WP-1's design-system job. Prefer padding and `minimumInteractiveComponentSize` over layout rewrites. **Do not** commit or push yourself.
